# Radio Vault v0.28.0-alpha16 — Actionable Research Workspace

Alpha 16 strips the Research & Metadata workspace back to understandable actions and integrates quality checking into the normal workflow.

## Changed

- Reduced primary Research navigation to Needs your decision, Broadcasts to find and Metadata editor.
- Removed the all-record Research library browser from normal use.
- Renamed and redesigned Missing broadcasts as Broadcasts to find.
- Reframed archive-health gap findings as useful discovery leads rather than evidence of lost files.
- Moved import history and technical diagnostics into clearly labelled advanced utilities.
- Renamed the old Quality checks surface to Advanced research diagnostics.

## Added

- Background research-quality maintenance when the workspace opens.
- Automatic application and logging of repairs already classified as safe.
- Plain-language actionable quality findings inside Needs your decision.
- Direct routing from a quality finding to the relevant metadata editor or unattached broadcast lead.
- Positive discovery status labels: Confirmed broadcast, Strong research lead and Broadcast lead.

## Preserved

- Alpha 15 rapid side-by-side conflict decisions and keyboard controls.
- Guarded quality-repair history and undo checks.
- Research-pack import/export and import rollback history.
- Database schema 45 and all existing export formats.
- Alpha 14 canonical playback and Library Truth completion behaviour.
