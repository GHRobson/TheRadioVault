# Library Truth Engine

The Library Truth pipeline is the canonical archive-ingestion and audit engine used by v0.28.

## Pipeline

1. Read physical inventory and preservation evidence.
2. Learn folder-level show/date conventions.
3. Parse show, date, slot, multipart and headline evidence.
4. Assemble filename-family-safe recording variants.
5. Rank complete, alternate, multipart, partial, fragment and damaged recordings.
6. Audit suspicious duration/programme families and cross-identity audio conflicts.
7. Persist direct and review-only Recording Segment → Broadcast Coverage evidence.
8. Calculate conservative readiness while retaining held and blocked cases.
9. Persist deterministic guarded adoption plans.
10. Rehearse eligible plans in a validated disposable clone and prove rollback.
11. Classify field-level metadata/reference differences with provenance and alternates.
12. Seal, back up, commit and independently verify adopted canonical structure.

## v0.28 safeguards

- Structural identity comes from Library Truth, not arbitrary duplicate episode rows.
- Filename-family and variant boundaries prevent unsafe multipart cross-pairing.
- Alternate recordings and exact copies remain preserved without duplicating broadcasts.
- Comparable contradictions and review-required coverage remain held.
- Every adopted operation and field decision remains auditable.
- Adoption is one-time, backed up, transactional, integrity-checked and fail-closed.
- Playback uses canonical manifests and refuses incomplete/review-required assemblies.
- Audio files are never renamed, moved or modified by adoption.
