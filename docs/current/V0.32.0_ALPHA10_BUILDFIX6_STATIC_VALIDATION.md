## Focused Dashboard scope

- The primary Dashboard grid uses a five-to-three split: continuation content on the left, with Surprise Me and a two-by-two stats group on the right.
- Surprise Me is presented as the WPF-inspired “Not sure what to play?” discovery card.
- On This Day retains the timed carousel and centred pagination dots, with a larger content area and explicit topic presentation.
- Recently Added and Unheard Broadcasts are matching half-width lists.
- `DashboardViewModel` loads five unheard broadcasts through `LibraryListeningFilter.Unplayed` and includes them in live playback/favourite synchronisation.
- No other screen layout was redesigned in this pass.
