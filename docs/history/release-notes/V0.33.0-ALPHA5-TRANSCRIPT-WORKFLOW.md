# Radio Vault 0.33.0 Alpha 5 — Transcript workflow acceptance

## Included

- Deep Research Packs contain `transcripts.json` with complete transcript text, timed segments and speaker labels.
- Search covers broadcast metadata, people, topics, Research content and transcript speech, with relevance ordering and match context.
- Library and Search broadcast menus can queue a full transcription without first playing the broadcast.
- The Transcripts workspace exposes Pause, Resume, Cancel and Retry controls for selected jobs.
- Pause suspends the native Whisper worker rather than discarding or restarting completed processing.

## Acceptance checks

1. Export a Deep Research Pack for a show that has transcripts; open the pack as a ZIP and confirm `transcripts.json` is present.
2. Search for an unusual phrase spoken in a transcript and confirm the matching broadcast appears with a Transcript excerpt.
3. Right-click a broadcast in Library or Search and choose **Transcribe broadcast**.
4. Select the running job in Transcripts, pause it, confirm the state reads Paused, then resume it.
5. Pause again and cancel; confirm the job finishes as Cancelled and can be retried.
