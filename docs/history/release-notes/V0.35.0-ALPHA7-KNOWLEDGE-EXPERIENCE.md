# Radio Vault 0.35.0 Alpha 7 — Knowledge experience

Alpha 7 turns the Wiki and Research areas into places for reading and exploration rather than collections of editing panels.

## Wiki

- Articles use an encyclopaedia-style information hierarchy: title, lead, contents and body first; a compact facts and image column second; related pages, chronology and numbered references after the article.
- Plain Markdown paragraphs now wrap naturally around inline internal links.
- The Wiki dashboard combines search, live database statistics, featured pages, history from this date, eras, shows, people and topics.

## Research and broadcast context

- Research opens on a database dashboard showing record count, archive range, shows, knowledge coverage, sources and outstanding work.
- Broadcast Info no longer repeats a generic Wiki exploration panel. People and topic chips remain direct Wiki links.
- The transcript reader follows Topics and uses the normal Radio Vault accent and surface colours.

## Discovery

- The main Dashboard adds Wiki context to Continue Listening and On this day without delaying the primary library load.
- Search uses rounded native surfaces, clearer filter groupings and consistent discovery cards.

## Verification

The release gate covers the new information hierarchy, Research dashboard, contextual Wiki summaries, Broadcast Info placement and Search styling.
