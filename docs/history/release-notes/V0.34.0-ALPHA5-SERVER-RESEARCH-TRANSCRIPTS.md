# Radio Vault v0.34.0 Alpha 5 - Server-owned Research and transcripts

Alpha 5 moves the native Research workspace, transcript records, speaker identities, transcription-job status and Deep Research Pack transfers behind the dedicated Radio Vault Server. The native client no longer opens those repositories directly.

## Acceptance

1. Start the matching Alpha 5 server, then the matching Alpha 5 native client.
2. Open Research and confirm its overview, records, sources, coverage and date-review views load normally.
3. Edit a Research record or review flag, leave the page, return and confirm the change remains.
4. Open Transcripts and confirm existing transcripts, phrases and speaker assignments load normally.
5. Start a five-minute transcription sample. Confirm it appears in Individual jobs and that pause, resume and cancel work while applicable.
6. Complete a transcription, edit one phrase or speaker assignment, reopen it and confirm the edit remains.
7. Export a Deep Research Pack from Research and confirm the summary includes its transcript count.
8. Preview that pack for import, then cancel the preview. Preview it again and complete the import.
9. Recheck Library playback and move playback between the native client and Anywhere to confirm Alpha 2-4 behaviour remains intact.

The Alpha 5 server and client are a matched pair. No database migration is performed.

## Remaining transition boundary

Whisper, diarization and voice-embedding processes still execute on the native computer that starts the work. Their authoritative transcript and job records are read and written through the server. Moving the workers themselves into the background server, and exposing the full Research/transcription UI in Radio Vault Anywhere, are subsequent roadmap slices.
