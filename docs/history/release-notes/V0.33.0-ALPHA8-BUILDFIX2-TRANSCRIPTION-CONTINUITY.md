# Radio Vault 0.33.0 Alpha 8 Buildfix 2

## Confirmed defect

The 2 April 2015 Ron & Fez transcription used the correct physical M4A file, but VAD skipped approximately 23 minutes of genuine speech and produced hallucinated repetition at the boundary. The same run returned 499 speaker identities from diarization. Diagnostic clips from 81:50 and 105:46 confirmed the source recording itself was correct.

## Corrections

- Full-broadcast and batch jobs always transcribe continuous audio. VAD is limited to selected ranges of 30 minutes or less.
- Converted range timestamps are mapped back to their original broadcast positions without heuristic rollover.
- Zero-duration, implausibly dense and heavily repetitive speech output becomes `[Unclear audio]`.
- The diarization clustering default is calibrated to 0.9; existing 0.5 defaults migrate automatically.
- Speaker results beyond a duration-based plausibility ceiling are retained only as diagnostic metadata and are not applied to transcript phrases.
- A completed current broadcast exposes **Re-transcribe full broadcast**. Its old transcript is replaced only after the new job succeeds.

## Acceptance

- Five-minute samples may use VAD; full jobs may not.
- Prepared samples retain their original Library timeline.
- A 499-speaker result for a 2-hour-40-minute recording is rejected.
- A failed replacement leaves the previous transcript untouched.
- The complete release gate passes with no compiler warnings or errors.
