## Focused correction scope

- The remaining Avalonia/Fluent teal selection states are replaced with Radio Vault's desaturated yellow pill tone for Library view toggles, selected rows and the persistent-player volume control.
- Sidebar selected frames retain a fixed width with additional right-side clearance so the complete yellow keyline remains visible beside the scrollbar.
- Library broadcast rows use a compact listening column and a flexible, clipped title region so narrow windows truncate titles cleanly rather than letting progress and title compete.
- The Moments detail panel clips its rounded header, and all diamond Moments glyphs are replaced with a bookmark-plus-time symbol.
- Navigation and playback actions use the established semantic key colours: yellow for archive/library, blue for listening/search, green for completion/connection, pink for favourites, orange for Moments and violet for Research.
- Research text and filter controls retain rounded Radio Vault surfaces on hover/focus instead of becoming black or square.
- Research opens in Needs attention and queries only review flags, unresolved conflicts and pending ambiguous reconciliation choices. All research remains available explicitly.
- An empty Needs-attention result presents a reassuring Everything is up to date state rather than implying that every archive record requires work.
- No database schema, LAN capability, playback-handoff or authoritative-audit ownership contract is changed.
