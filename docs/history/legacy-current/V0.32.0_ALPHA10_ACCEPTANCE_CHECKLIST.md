# Radio Vault v0.32.0 Alpha 10 acceptance checklist

## Build and startup

- `BUILD-AND-RUN.cmd` restores, builds and launches `TheRadioVault.exe`.
- Existing schema-45 Library opens without migration or re-adoption.
- Startup and shutdown leave no hidden process or progress loss.

## Identity and themes

- Yellow is the primary accent in System, Light and Dark modes.
- Theme selection persists after restart.
- Text, focus and selected states remain legible in both themes.
- Purple legacy accent and unexplained grey icon tiles are absent from normal Avalonia views.

## Dashboard and Library

- On This Day advances automatically and the centred active dot follows it.
- Dot selection works directly and On This Day topics are visible.
- Surprise Me is near the primary listening actions; Recently Added contains at most five items.
- Active playback updates Dashboard and Library progress without Refresh.
- Hearts, row highlight, hover play/pause and compact progress bars work in list and chronological broadcast lists.
- Year and month listened percentages are accurate after reopening the period view.
- Dashboard cards and continuation/recent rows have visible restrained outlines; Library broadcast rows, period rows and the selected-broadcast details pane are framed.
- The active-playing Library row keeps its full outline as well as the stronger yellow left edge.
- Other card-based screens retain visible grouping; padding and margins remain compact enough for normal laptop windows.

## Research

- Records and Metadata Studio remain usable at normal laptop window sizes.
- Search/filtering does not crowd the record list.
- Local import previews before applying, creates a pre-import database backup and protects manual fields.
- Remote import/export runs on the server and never writes to the client SQLite cache.
- Exported `.trvpack` opens with the existing WPF importer.
- An `authoritative_audit` pack displays an explicit warning in both Avalonia and WPF/server preview flows.
- Importing one audited record with intentional blanks clears only the corresponding stale research-owned fields and replaces its durable sources, people, topics and research moments.
- An ambiguous audited identity remains unapplied and review-only; playback history, favourites, Queue and personal Moments are unchanged.

## Playback handoff

Complete every row in `V0.32.0_ALPHA10_THREE_DEVICE_HANDOFF_MATRIX.md`, including multipart, queue, stale-device and reconnect tests.

## Regression

- Local and remote playback, favourites, Queue, Moments, Search, Research, Metadata Studio and Connected Access still work.
- Rubber-band scrolling remains responsive without jitter or delayed snap-back.
- WPF reference remains usable as the authoritative server and recovery shell.
