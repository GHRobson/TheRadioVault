# Radio Vault 0.35.0 Alpha 3 — Wiki exploration and reading

Alpha 3 turns the Wiki into an archive-exploration feature rather than opening directly into an editor. It retains the human-authoring and portable `.rvwiki` foundation from Alpha 2 while adding complete reading, discovery, evidence review and archive navigation.

## Included

- A native Wiki dashboard with featured pages, recently updated articles, shows, people, topics and timeline-led starting points.
- A dedicated reading mode with rendered Markdown, contents navigation, internal Wiki links and dated/captioned article images.
- Exact timeline and citation playback links that retain the canonical `EpisodeId`, optional `MomentId` and timestamp. Headlines are display labels and are not used as identity.
- Relevant Wiki-page panels on Library, Broadcast Info, Now Playing, Search and Research.
- Clickable people and topic chips throughout the native client and Radio Vault Web. Exact page titles/slugs open directly; a missing exact page opens the matching Wiki search instead of guessing.
- Revision history with comparisons and safe restoration as a new revision, leaving the historical record intact.
- A read-focused Radio Vault Web Wiki with its own navigation entry, dashboard, search, Markdown reader, contents, images, sources and exact archive playback links.
- A baseline citation audit that runs automatically after `.rvwiki` import and can be rerun manually. It identifies uncited substantial articles, numbering gaps, unused citations, missing sources, Web sources without URLs, archive citations without stable broadcast identity and weak source locators.

## AI baseline workflow

1. Generate archive-aware starter pages, then export an `.rvwiki` authoring pack from the native client.
2. Give that pack to the selected AI agent. The pack contains article files, structured evidence, existing human revisions and a read-only archive catalogue; it does not expose private filesystem paths.
3. Require the agent to preserve page IDs and base revisions, cite factual claims in Wikipedia style, link broadcast evidence with `EpisodeId`/`BroadcastUid`, and return a valid `.rvwiki` pack.
4. Preview the returned pack in Radio Vault. Review every Added, Changed, Unchanged and Protected page before applying it.
5. Apply the pack. Radio Vault protects newer human revisions and immediately runs the citation audit. Resolve every error and review every warning before publishing the baseline.

The AI-authored content is archive-specific and is deliberately not fabricated or bundled with the application. Alpha 3 supplies the safe export, preview, import, revision-protection and citation-audit workflow required to create it from the user's real archive.

## Acceptance checks

1. Open Wiki and confirm the exploration dashboard appears before editing controls.
2. Open a page containing Markdown, internal links and an image; confirm headings, contents, image, caption and date render in reading mode.
3. Click a person or topic chip from Dashboard, Library, Broadcast Info, Now Playing and Radio Vault Web; confirm the exact Wiki page opens or a matching Wiki search appears.
4. Open a timeline event linked to a broadcast and a Moment; confirm each starts the canonical broadcast at its stored timestamp.
5. Edit and save a page twice, compare its revisions, restore the older one and confirm restoration creates a new revision.
6. Import a cited `.rvwiki` pack and confirm the automatic citation audit appears. Verify deliberately broken citation numbering and a Broadcast source without archive identity are reported.
7. Open the same article in Radio Vault Web and confirm Markdown, contents, images, citations and exact playback links are readable on desktop and phone layouts.
