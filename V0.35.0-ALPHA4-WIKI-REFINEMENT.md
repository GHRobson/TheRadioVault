# Radio Vault 0.35.0 Alpha 4 - Wiki refinement

Alpha 4 turns the Wiki from an authoring workspace into a connected place to explore the archive.

## Navigation and reading

- Wiki Home, Browse, Article and Timeline Explorer are separate destinations.
- Article pages use the full reading area; the complete page catalogue only appears in Browse.
- Back and Forward preserve page-to-page exploration like a web browser.
- Search occupies the main toolbar and offers matching-page suggestions.
- Create, edit, starter generation, import, export, audit and refresh actions live under the three-dot menu.
- Reading is the default. Editing remains an explicit action and retains revision protection.

## Connected articles

- Explicit Wiki Markdown links and plain mentions of existing page titles render as inline links.
- Unresolved links are marked as missing and open useful Wiki search results.
- Every article can show related pages, backlinks and an at-a-glance infobox.
- Images retain captions, dates, credits and licences; citations show author, URL, locator, notes and supporting quotations.

## Exploration and timelines

- The dashboard includes featured pages, shows, people, topics, recent changes, eras and On this date.
- Timeline Explorer lets the reader choose a show, scrub through its years, scan nearby event cards and play linked broadcasts or Moments.
- Timeline editing exposes staged broadcast and Moment links and allows individual links to be removed before saving.

## Quality and parity

- The Wiki quality audit combines citation checks with broken-link, duplicate-page and orphan-page detection.
- Radio Vault Web has the same dashboard concepts, Back/Forward navigation, article infoboxes, inline links, related pages, backlinks and responsive Timeline Explorer.
- Web article reading collapses cleanly to one column on phones.

## Acceptance checks

1. Open Wiki Home, select two linked pages, then use Back and Forward.
2. Confirm the page catalogue is absent while reading an article and returns only when Browse is selected.
3. Search for a person and use a suggested result.
4. Open Timeline Explorer, select a show, drag across years and play a linked broadcast or Moment.
5. Verify an existing page title mentioned in prose becomes a link and a missing explicit link is visibly marked.
6. Run Wiki quality from the three-dot menu and inspect each issue category.
7. Repeat Home, article navigation and Timeline Explorer in Radio Vault Web at desktop and phone widths.
