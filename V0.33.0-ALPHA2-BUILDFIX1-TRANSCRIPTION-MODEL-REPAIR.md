# Radio Vault 0.33.0 Alpha 2 Buildfix 1 acceptance

This buildfix repairs the two transcription setup failures found during the first laptop test. The TinyDiarize model now downloads from the separate source selected by whisper.cpp's official model-download scripts, and ordinary models cannot retain the incompatible TinyDiarize option.

## Acceptance check

1. In **Settings → Transcription**, choose **Small English TinyDiarize** and select **Selected model only**.
2. Confirm `ggml-small.en-tdrz.bin` downloads without a 404 and becomes the configured model.
3. Confirm the TinyDiarize option is available and enabled for that model.
4. Select or browse to an ordinary model such as `ggml-small.en.bin`.
5. Confirm TinyDiarize is cleared and cannot be enabled for the incompatible model.
6. Play a broadcast, create a five-minute sample, and confirm the job starts rather than failing immediately.

The worker, model and VAD remain on demand and transcription remains local to this PC.
