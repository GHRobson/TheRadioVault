# Radio Vault 0.33.0 Alpha 7 — Transcript review and remembered voices

## Included

- Correct transcript wording and save a new durable transcript revision.
- Split a selected phrase at its nearest middle word and timestamp.
- Merge consecutive phrases only when they belong to the same speaker.
- Mark phrases reviewed or return them to the needs-attention state.
- Search the open transcript by wording or speaker and step through every match.
- Confirm one name across an anonymous diarized speaker cluster.
- Learn confirmed voices locally from the already-installed speaker embedding model.
- Offer high-confidence remembered-voice matches as suggestions that still require confirmation.
- Export TXT transcripts, SRT subtitles and WebVTT captions through the standard save window.

## Acceptance checks

1. Open a completed multi-speaker transcript and select a phrase.
2. Correct its wording, choose **Save wording**, switch transcripts and return; confirm the correction remains.
3. Choose **Split phrase**, then merge the two halves with **Merge next**.
4. Toggle **Mark reviewed** and **Mark as needs attention** and confirm the row state changes.
5. Search for a phrase or speaker and use **Previous** and **Next** to move through matches; play the selected result.
6. Enter a speaker name or choose a person from the broadcast, then choose **Confirm voice**; confirm every phrase from that anonymous speaker changes to the name.
7. After confirming at least two clear samples for a person, open another transcript containing that voice and confirm a confidence-marked suggestion appears when the local match is strong.
8. Export TXT, SRT and VTT and open each file to confirm timestamps, wording and confirmed names are present.
