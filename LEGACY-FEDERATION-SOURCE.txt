Radio Vault 1.0 networking boundary
===================================

The embedded Radio Vault Anywhere web/PWA server is active again.

Native Avalonia federation remains postponed until after 1.0:
- no desktop remote-client mode;
- no LAN discovery;
- no desktop pairing;
- no remote Library cache;
- no desktop-to-desktop streaming;
- no device ownership or handoff.

The old federation source remains in this archive as reference material, but
none of its client-side services are linked into the active Avalonia project.
