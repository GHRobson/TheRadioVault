# Radio Vault 0.35.0 Alpha 6 - Unified archive knowledge

Alpha 6 replaces separate Research and Wiki exchange files with one `.trvknowledge` Archive Knowledge Database. It is a portable SQLite database designed to be inspected or edited by an AI research agent and then safely imported into Radio Vault.

## What to test

1. Open Research and export an Archive Knowledge Database for the whole archive or a selected show/year.
2. Confirm the file opens as SQLite and includes Research, transcripts, Wiki pages, sources, citations, images, timeline events and `knowledge_links`.
3. Import the same database and review the combined Research/Wiki preview before applying it.
4. Open the Wiki Timeline Explorer, choose a show and scroll vertically through the complete history.
5. Open a Wiki page and use an archive-backed source, timeline broadcast or Moment link; playback should start at the preserved position.
6. Open Broadcast Info for a broadcast with a transcript and expand **Read transcript**. Selecting a phrase should play that exact time.
7. Confirm Transcription Studio is under Research rather than a separate sidebar destination.
8. Restart the client and confirm the redesigned splash remains visible for at least three seconds while cached startup data is prepared.
9. Repeat the Wiki, Timeline, Research database and Transcription Studio checks in Radio Vault Web.

## Compatibility

Old `.trvpack` and `.rvwiki` imports are intentionally not supported by this Alpha. The database schema used by the installed archive is unchanged; only the portable knowledge exchange format is replaced.

Client and Server are a matched Alpha 6 pair. LAN capability generation is 40 and the Radio Vault Web shell cache is v65.
